# Notice: real-data numbers in this directory

Two result sets in this directory come from real bank transactions, **262 transactions of one contributing company**
(June to September 2026), labelled blind by one person, evaluated with the product's candidate list:

- `kor07/B7/vegleges.json`, `vegleges.md` (round 07B: the frozen synthetic-calibrated models on real items);
- `kor08/V/v_ertekeles.*`, `kor08/V/kozvetlen/v_ertekeles.*` (round 08 K08V: the chain on real items).

These are **development numbers, aggregates only:** the sample is known, the labels are known, and in K08V the
threshold was chosen on this sample, so the error rates are optimistic. They do not count as a confirmatory result.
Per-item data, labels and the labelling sheets are not in this package. The confirmatory check is a fresh, pre-registered sample
of about 150 items (see `../decision-record.md`).
